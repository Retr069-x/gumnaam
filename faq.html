Questions, quietly.

What does Gumnaam mean?+

Nameless, in Urdu. Unknown by name. Known by presence.

How much is delivery?+

PKR 200 once per order across Pakistan, with COD or Easypaisa. One PKR 1,999 hoodie totals PKR 2,199.

Is Easypaisa confirmed automatically?+

No. Transfer the displayed total, then submit the transaction ID and screenshot. We check the funds in our account and confirm manually on WhatsApp. Never share a PIN, password or OTP.

Can I order more than one piece?+

Add each piece and size to your bag. Change quantities there. Delivery is charged once, not per hoodie. Availability is confirmed before dispatch.

Will there be a restock?+

Four pieces. No restock promised.

How do I change an order?+

Contact us with your order reference before dispatch. We will check what is still possible.

How should I care for the fleece?+

Cold wash inside out. Line dry. Do not iron the mark. Check the care notes on your product page.
