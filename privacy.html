Privacy & payments

Your information

We collect your name, Pakistan mobile number, delivery address, selected piece and size. Email and delivery notes are optional. We use these details to review, fulfil and confirm your order on WhatsApp. Checkout does not subscribe you to marketing.

Orders are stored in Supabase. New-order alerts are sent to Gumnaam through Resend. Delivery details are shared with the courier when needed to send your parcel. Our spam check uses Cloudflare Turnstile.

Easypaisa

Transfer to Abdul Muneeb, account 03041149722. Review your total first. Upload the transaction ID and a payment screenshot. Hide your wallet balance and unrelated transactions.

Screenshots are held in private storage. The order stays pending while we check the transaction in our account. A screenshot alone does not confirm payment. We do not request your PIN, password or OTP.

Cash on delivery

Each hoodie is PKR 1,999. COD adds PKR 200 once per order. One hoodie with COD totals PKR 2,199. The charge is not applied to Easypaisa orders. The full total appears before submission.

Confirmation & corrections

Placing an order submits a request, not a guarantee of stock or dispatch. We confirm availability, payment and delivery on WhatsApp. Contact us before dispatch to correct details, request cancellation or discuss a return or refund.

Access & deletion

To request a correction or deletion, email gumnaamofficialpk@gmail.com with your order reference. We retain information needed to fulfil orders, resolve payment disputes and meet applicable recordkeeping requirements. Payment images should be removed once no longer needed.
